## About me

I'm an ML / Data Analyst working at the intersection of **generative AI, statistical evaluation, and data-centric model improvement**. My focus is understanding not only *which* model performs better, but also *where it fails, why it fails, and what can be improved*.

My work includes evaluation of **image, video, and synchronized audio-video generation**, human-preference studies, failure-mode analysis, and assessing the quality of data used for model training.

## What I focus on

- **Generative AI evaluation:** image and video generation, prompt adherence, visual quality, multimodal consistency, and human side-by-side comparisons.
- **Rigorous experimentation:** evaluation design, metric interpretation, statistical confidence, and decision-oriented reporting.
- **Root cause & error analysis:** identifying weak scenarios, building error taxonomies, and connecting model behavior to potential data issues.

- **Practical ML:** reproducible experiments, feature engineering, classification, regression, and neural network optimization.

## Selected projects

### 🔎 [Fact-Level Image Caption Evaluation](https://github.com/OllaAmigos/image2text_captioning_evaluation_fact_level)
A **de-identified evaluation framework** for image-to-text captions, built around importance-weighted factual precision and recall, extraction quality gates, text-in-image checks, error analysis, and bootstrap confidence intervals. Includes an executable **synthetic-data demo** and tests; no proprietary evaluation results are published.

`Python` · `LLM/VLM evaluation` · `Data quality` · `Bootstrap CI`

### 🧠 [SWAMP & Iterative Magnitude Pruning](https://github.com/OllaAmigos/swamp-iterative-pruning)
Collaborative research reproduction comparing **iterative magnitude pruning**, IMP ensembles, and SWAMP on CIFAR-10. My contributions included IMP baselines and ensembles, literature analysis, and interpretation of model-size/quality trade-offs.

`PyTorch` · `CIFAR-10` · `Neural network pruning` · `Research reproduction`

### 📈 [Uplift Modeling with Kalman Filtering](https://github.com/OllaAmigos/Uplift-Kalman_pmmo)
An exploratory collaborative study combining transaction-based feature engineering, **linear state-space modeling**, Kalman filtering, and uplift modeling to investigate customer response across different populations. The cross-population uplift estimates are **research hypotheses**, not validated causal effects.

`Python` · `Time-series / panel data` · `Kalman filter` · `Uplift modeling`

### 📊 [Tabular ML Case Studies](https://github.com/OllaAmigos/tabular-ml-case-studies)
Reproducible baselines for **wine-quality classification** and **diamond-price regression**, with preprocessing pipelines and explicit labeled holdout evaluation. Results on one held-out split: **ROC AUC 0.817** and **R² 0.963**, respectively (not external validation).

`scikit-learn` · `Classification` · `Regression` · `Pipelines`

### 🐍 [Python ML Foundations](https://github.com/OllaAmigos/python-ml-foundations)
Small, testable **from-scratch implementations** of bag-of-words, TF–IDF, and CSV grouping/statistics using the Python standard library.

`Python` · `Text vectorization` · `Unit testing` · `Algorithms`
